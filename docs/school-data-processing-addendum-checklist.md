# School Data Processing Addendum Checklist

Use this checklist with qualified counsel before signing a school agreement. It records the operational terms SMARTCoach can support; it is not a substitute for legal review.

## Parties and Roles

- Identify the school or district as the controller/owner of school-submitted data.
- Identify the legal SMARTCoach contracting entity and notice address.
- Define SMARTCoach as a service provider/processor acting on documented school instructions.
- State that school athlete data is not sold or used for behavioral advertising.

## Data and Purpose

- Describe roster identifiers, team grouping, attendance, training plans, performance results, meet results, equipment records, and coach notes selected by the school.
- Describe coach account, billing, authorized-device, request, and security-log data.
- Limit processing to operating, securing, supporting, and improving the contracted service.

## Security Commitments

- HTTPS transport and provider-managed encryption at rest.
- Signed, expiring sessions and account-scoped authorization.
- Restricted Power Trak rack sessions and authorized-device revocation.
- Authentication rate limiting, mutation audit records, and incident response procedures.
- Staff confidentiality obligations and least-privilege production access.

## School Controls

- Authorized contacts and coach account administration.
- Data export, correction, deletion, and account termination procedures.
- Required response times for verified school requests.
- School responsibility for credential sharing, roster accuracy, and lawful notices or consents.

## Vendors and Transfers

- Attach a current subprocessor list with service purpose and processing location.
- Define notice and objection procedure for material subprocessor changes.
- Address cross-border transfers if any school requires them.

## Retention and Incidents

- Attach the production retention schedule by data category.
- Define deletion timing after account termination, including backup aging.
- Define security-incident notice contacts, timing, investigation cooperation, and remediation reporting.

## Assurance

- State whether independent penetration testing, vulnerability scanning, or SOC reports are available.
- Do not claim FERPA, COPPA, state student-privacy, SOC 2, ISO 27001, or district-policy compliance until counsel or an independent assessor confirms the applicable scope.
- Record agreement version, approval owner, review date, and renewal review date.
