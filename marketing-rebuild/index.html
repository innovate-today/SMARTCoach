BUILT FROM COACHING

Not dreamed up in a software company.

SMARTCoach grew out of years of coaching cross country and track: writing splits, planning workouts, managing athletes, working with assistant coaches, and trying to keep track of everything that comes with running a program.

The goal was not to build more technology. It was to make the information coaches already collect more useful.

“The information usually existed. The problem was that it lived in different places and with different coaches.”
