Privacy and Data Practices

Effective September 21, 2026

SMARTCoach is a coach-directed team training service. Schools and authorized coaching staff decide what team information is entered and how it is used.

Information SMARTCoach Handles

Depending on the features a school enables, SMARTCoach may process coach account details, athlete roster identifiers, graduation year or grade, event group, attendance, workouts, training results, meet results, equipment records, and coach notes. Power Trak stores workout assignments and exercise results such as exercise, repetitions, weight, height, time, and related testing values.

SMARTCoach also records limited technical and security information, including authorized device identifiers, sign-in events, request outcomes, browser information, and timestamps. Billing contacts and subscription status may be processed for account administration.

How Information Is Used

  • Provide team planning, athlete tracking, testing, reporting, and synchronization features.
  • Operate account access, authorized-device controls, backups, support, and security monitoring.
  • Maintain subscriptions and communicate with authorized school or coaching contacts.
  • Diagnose failures and improve reliability.

SMARTCoach does not use school athlete data for behavioral advertising and does not sell school athlete data.

School Control and Access

The school or team remains responsible for authorizing coaches, choosing the information entered, and managing roster access. Coach sessions expire, devices can be signed out by authorized staff, and restricted Power Trak rack sessions cannot open the broader coaching system.

Service Providers and Integrations

SMARTCoach uses service providers to host the application, store account-scoped records, administer subscriptions, and support configured integrations. Depending on the school's setup, these may include Vercel, Redis-compatible managed storage, GoHighLevel/LeadConnector, Stripe, and optional Strava connectivity. Each optional integration is used only when configured or authorized for that account. A current provider list can be requested through the school's SMARTCoach service contact.

Retention and Deletion

Operational records are retained while needed to provide the service and support the school account. Authorized school contacts may request account export, correction, or deletion through their SMARTCoach service contact. Security records may be retained for a limited period when needed to investigate misuse, meet contractual obligations, or protect the service.

Security

SMARTCoach uses signed, expiring sessions; account-scoped authorization; device revocation; restricted rack-device access; rate limiting; security event logging; encrypted HTTPS transport; and browser security controls. No system can guarantee absolute security. Schools should keep coach credentials private, promptly remove former staff, and report suspected access issues.

Student and Athlete Requests

Athletes and families should direct requests about school-entered information to their school or team. SMARTCoach will work with the authorized school contact on verified access, correction, export, and deletion requests.

Changes and Contact

Material changes to these practices will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date. Privacy, security, or data requests should be sent through the SMARTCoach service contact listed in the school's agreement or account onboarding materials.
